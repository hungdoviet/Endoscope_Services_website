Precision repair services for gastroscopes used in gastrointestinal procedures.

Expert Gastroscope Repair & Rebuild Services

Gastroscopes are utilized in high-frequency diagnostic and therapeutic procedures, demanding pristine optical clarity and ultra-responsive maneuverability. At Endoscope Services, based in Noble Park, Victoria, we deliver expert repair and servicing solutions for gastrovideoscope models from all primary manufacturers. Our technicians are specialized in restoring high-definition video signals, resolving fluid ingress, and repairing delicate mechanical assemblies to ensure maximum uptime for your clinic.

Common Repairs

  • Angulation System Overhaul: Precision wire replacement, guide mesh rebuilding, and tension calibration to ensure accurate 4-way angulation control for optimal upper GI access.
  • Air/Water Nozzle Maintenance: Clearing chronic blockages, replacing distal nozzles, and sealing joints to ensure reliable lens cleaning during procedures.
  • Insertion Tube Re-coating: Re-lacquering and smoothing insertion tubes to eliminate surface scratches and bite marks, reducing patient discomfort.
  • Suction Channel Replacement: Installing new, medical-grade suction channels to restore peak aspiration rate and clear blockages.
  • Light Guide Tube Repair: Replacing or re-terminating damaged light guide tubes and connectors to restore high light output.

Specialized Diagnostics & Rebuilds

  • CCD Camera Reconstruction: Rebuilding image sensors, resolving "snowy" video feeds, and re-soldering video logic boards.
  • One-Touch Connector Repair: Full sealing and PCB repair within the connector plug assembly for complete waterproofing.
  • Light Guide Fiber Bundle Repair: Aligning and replacing individual glass fibers in the optical bundle to restore optimal illumination.
  • Distal Lens Restoration: Precision polishing and vacuum-sealing of the objective lens to prevent fogging and blurry views.
  • Helium Leak Testing: High-sensitivity leak verification to identify micro-fractures before fluid invades the inner scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does gastroscope repair take?

Minor repairs are typically completed in 2 to 3 business days, while major rebuilds (such as insertion tube or CCD camera replacement) take 3 to 5 business days, depending on parts availability.

What are the most common gastroscope issues you repair?

We frequently fix angulation system issues, air/water channel blockages, fluid invasion leaks, light guide bundle replacements, and distal lens scratches.

Do you support Olympus GIF-H190 and GIF-HQ190 gastroscopes?

Yes, we specialize in high-definition gastroscope models from Olympus, Pentax, and Fujifilm, using high-quality OEM-compatible components to ensure precise handling.

Is a satisfaction guarantee included?

Every gastroscope repair is backed by a 90-day warranty and our 7-day satisfaction guarantee to ensure absolute clinical safety and device longevity.
