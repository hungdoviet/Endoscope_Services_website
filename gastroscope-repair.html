Restoration Scope

Reliable Gastroscope Maintenance & Repair

A functioning gastroscope is critical for accurate diagnostic visualization and procedural safety. Based in Noble Park, Melbourne, our biomedical facility provides rapid, component-level repairs to OEM specifications with zero compromise on quality.

Common Repairs

  • Angulation System Overhaul: Precision tensioning and wire replacement to ensure accurate 4-way direction control for upper GI access.
  • Air/Water Nozzle Maintenance: Clearing chronic blockages and replacing nozzles to ensure reliable lens cleaning.
  • Insertion Tube Re-coating: Fixing surface damage and restoring the smooth, low-friction finish required for comfortable insertion.
  • Suction Channel Replacement: Installing new, high-grade internal tubing to restore peak suction performance.

Specialized Services

  • CCD Camera Reconstruction: Fixing image artifacts, "snowy" video, or complete loss of imaging signal.
  • One-Touch Connector Repair: Sealing and PCB repair within the connector body for complete waterproofing.
  • Light Guide Fiber Bundle Repair: Re-aligning or replacing optical fibers to eliminate shadow spots in the image.
  • Distal Lens Restoration: Polishing and sealing the distal lens to prevent fogging and blurry imaging.
Engineering Protocol

Step-by-Step Technical Repair & Overhaul Procedures

Gastroscopes require precise optical, mechanical, and electronic restoration to navigate the upper GI tract safely. Every repair at our Melbourne workshop follows rigorous step-by-step protocols:

01

Wet Pressure Leak Diagnostics & Disassembly

Each gastroscope undergoes pressurized nitrogen leak testing under distilled water to detect hairline cracks in the bending rubber, light guide umbilical, or biopsy ports. Preventing internal moisture corrosion protects high-definition CCD camera sensors and video logic boards from permanent failure.

02

Angulation Mechanism & Pulley Alignment

Precise up/down (210°/90°) and left/right (100°/100°) deflection is critical for retroflexion in the stomach. We replace stretched steel control cables, realign internal drive pulleys inside the control handle, and adjust friction locks to eliminate steering slop or stiffness.

03

Air/Water Nozzle & Dual Channel Overhaul

Blockages or reduced pressure in the air/water nozzle prevent effective lens washing. We clean and realign micro-nozzles, replace internal PTFE fluid tubing, and verify high-pressure water jet delivery for clearing blood and mucous during gastroscopic procedures.

04

High-Definition Optical & Video Calibration

Micro-scratches on the objective lens are polished using optical abrasives. Damaged light guide fibers are replaced with high-luminance fiber optic bundles to eliminate dark spots. Finally, video signal stability and color reproduction are calibrated against broadcast standards.

Quality Assurance

Our 40-Point Diagnostic Inspection Process

Every gastroscope submitted to our workshop undergoes a rigorous 40-point safety and optical inspection before and after servicing:

Mechanical & Pressure Testing

  • Dry pressure leak diagnostic under distilled water immersion.
  • 4-way angulation control tension & deflection angle check.
  • Air/water & suction line flow dynamics and pressure testing.
  • Insertion tube surface smoothness & outer boot sealing inspection.

Optical & Digital Signal Integrity

  • HDTV image clarity, color fidelity, and sensor noise evaluation.
  • Light guide fiber bundle lux transmission measurement.
  • Objective lens scratch & fogging assessment under magnification.
  • Video connector electrical pin continuity & waterproof seal check.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Gastroscope Repair

Key information regarding turnaround times, fluid leak recovery, supported brands, and warranty protection.

How long does a gastroscope repair typically take?

Routine maintenance and minor component adjustments are completed in 24–48 hours. Major rebuilds, including channel replacements or optical bundle overhauls, take 3–5 business days. Fast-dispatched loaner gastroscopes are available to eliminate procedure cancellations.

What causes a gastroscope lens to fog, and how is it fixed?

Lens fogging is caused by fluid invasion through micro-cracks in the distal end glue or bending rubber. We repair this by clearing moisture, polishing the objective lens, replacing damaged seals, and curing new medical-grade epoxy under cleanroom conditions.

What gastroscope models do you repair?

We repair all major diagnostic and therapeutic gastroscopes, specializing in Olympus (GIF-HQ190, GIF-H190, GIF-190, GIF-XP190N, GIF-H180, GIF-Q180), Pentax (i10 & K series), and Fujifilm (700 & 600 series) scopes.

What are the most common signs that a gastroscope needs servicing?

Warning signs include sticky or loose angulation dials, loss of light guide brightness (shadow spots), air/water nozzle blockage, persistent fluid leak test bubbles, or pixelation/noise in the digital video output.

Do you provide a warranty on gastroscope overhauls?

Yes. Every gastroscope service is backed by our comprehensive warranty: 90 days for standard repairs and up to 8 months for major overhauls, covering replaced parts (like light guide bundles, biopsy channels, or bending rubbers) and technician craftsmanship.
