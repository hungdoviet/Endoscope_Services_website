Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment repair services do you provide?

We provide complete repair, rebuild, and preventative maintenance for flexible endoscopes (colonoscopes, gastroscopes, bronchoscopes, specialty video scopes like CYF-VH, ENF-V3, URF-V3) and surgical power tools.

Do you provide loaner equipment while our scopes are being serviced?

Yes, we offer complimentary loaner scopes to ensure zero clinical downtime during repair periods.
