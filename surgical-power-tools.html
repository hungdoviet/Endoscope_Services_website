Precision Surgical Power Tool Repairs

Surgical power tools, including drills, sagittal saws, and reamers, are critical for orthopedic and trauma surgery. These tools must operate at high speed and torque, meaning they require regular maintenance, load testing, and seal replacement. At Endoscope Services in Noble Park, Victoria, we offer complete repair, rebuilding, and calibration services for pneumatic, electric, and battery-powered surgical power tools from all major manufacturers, ensuring they function reliably under surgical load.

Types of Tools Serviced

  • Drills and Reamers: High-torque bone drills, pin drivers, and dual-trigger reamers serviced with new bearings and gears.
  • Saws: Sagittal, oscillating, and reciprocating saws rebuilt to restore accurate blade tracking and eliminate motor drag.
  • Attachments and Accessories: Standard chucks, wire drivers, angled attachments, and couplers fully cleaned and lubricated.
  • Battery Packs and Chargers: Battery testing, cells rebuilding, and console diagnostics to verify voltage and battery capacity.
  • Pneumatic and Electric Cords: Cable insulation verification, connector pins restoration, and hose leak check.

Our Rigorous Quality Verification

  • RPM and Torque Testing: Verifying motor rotation speed and torque limits under load to ensure optimal drilling/sawing capacity.
  • Bearings & Seals Overhaul: Replacing worn-out high-speed bearings and autoclaving-grade gaskets to prevent internal steam damage.
  • Pneumatic Pressure Leak Testing: Verifying high-pressure valves and hoses to prevent oil spray or pressure drops during clinical use.
  • Electrical Isolation Safety Check: Confirming grounding and insulation resistance on all electric tools.
  • Calibrated Load Simulation: Every handpiece is tested using simulated loads to confirm mechanical resilience before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which surgical power tool brands do you service?

We repair and service battery, electric, and pneumatic tools from all major manufacturers, including Stryker (System 5, 6, 7, 8), Hall (ConMed), Linvatec, MicroAire, and Synthes.

What types of repairs do you perform on surgical drills and saws?

We handle complete motor rebuilds, gear assembly repairs, high-speed bearing replacements, console/cord repairs, sealing gasket overhauls, and coupling adjustments.

How do you verify tool safety and calibration?

Every surgical power tool undergoes rigorous load testing, torque measurement, RPM verification, and electrical safety testing to ensure it meets strict clinical standards.

What is the turnaround time for power tool repairs?

Our typical turnaround time is 3 to 5 business days. Emergency expedite services are available for urgent facility needs, subject to parts availability.
