Restoration Scope

Comprehensive Colonoscope Servicing & Rebuilding

Colonoscopes undergo intense mechanical stress and chemical reprocessing. Based at our Noble Park workshop, our certified biomedical technicians deliver precision repairs from minor sealing adjustments to complete instrument rebuilds.

Common Repairs

  • Insertion Tube Replacement: Fixes kinks, bites, and deep scratches using OEM-grade materials with RIT (Responsive Insertion Technology) optimization.
  • Bending Section Re-build: Complete re-meshing and angulation wire tensioning to restore precise control and maneuverability.
  • Biopsy & Suction Channel Replacement: Installation of high-durability internal channels to resolve leaks and blockages.
  • Image Sensor (CCD / CMOS) Repair: Restoration of high-definition imaging, color balance, and electronic connector stability.
  • Air/Water Channel Clearing: High-pressure flushing and nozzle replacement to ensure optimal lens cleaning during procedures.

Specialized Services

  • Leak Testing & Seal Repair: Comprehensive diagnostic to prevent fluid invasion and internal corrosion.
  • Light Guide Bundle Replacement: Restore optimal illumination for crystal-clear diagnostics.
  • Distal End Polishing: Removing micro-scratches from the objective lens for peak optical performance.
  • Video Connector Re-termination: Repairing damaged pins and logic boards within the one-touch connector.
Engineering Protocol

Step-by-Step Technical Repair & Overhaul Procedures

At our specialized Noble Park workshop, every colonoscope overhaul follows strict biomedical engineering protocols to guarantee compliance with original manufacturer performance metrics:

01

Decontamination & Immersion Pressure Diagnostics

Before disassembling any instrument, the colonoscope undergoes high-level enzymatic decontamination followed by dry nitrogen pressure testing under distilled water. Submersion diagnostics allow technicians to isolate micro-perforations in the outer bending rubber, insertion tube sheath, or control knob seals, preventing fluid invasion into delicate internal camera sensors and logic boards.

02

Insertion Tube & RIT Calibration Procedure

When an insertion tube displays severe chemical degradation, surface buckling, or internal channel collapse, technicians desolder the distal camera electronics and extract internal channels. A new OEM-spec insertion tube featuring Responsive Insertion Technology (RIT) is refitted. RIT calibration ensures progressive flexibility along the shaft, giving gastroenterologists optimum torque transmission and colon navigation control.

03

4-Way Bending Mesh & Angulation Wire Re-building

Precise steering depends on four stainless steel angulation guidance wires housed within a flexible skeletal mesh. We replace worn wire cables, fit new stainless mesh sheaths, and set exact cable tension on the control handle pulleys. The bending section rubber is then re-sheathed and epoxy sealed using medical-grade hydrophobic compound to prevent fluid entry during hospital automated endoscope reprocessors (AER).

04

Optic Bundle & CCD Digital Sensor Calibration

Optical alignment is performed under high-power stereomicroscopes. Darkened or broken fiber optic illumination bundles are replaced with high-lux glass fibers. Image sensor chips (CCD/CMOS) are tested for color fidelity, gain balance, and pixel noise. Distal objective lenses are micro-polished to eliminate scratch artifacts and ensure crystal-clear video feed output during colonoscopies.

Quality Assurance

Our 40-Point Diagnostic Inspection Process

Every colonoscope submitted to our workshop undergoes a mandatory 40-point safety and performance verification checklist before and after repair:

Mechanical & Structural Checks

  • Continuous dry pressure leak test under distilled water immersion.
  • Angular deflection range measurement (Up/Down, Left/Right angles).
  • Biopsy channel insertion resistance gauge testing.
  • Outer insertion tube surface integrity & RIT stiffness inspection.

Optical & Electrical Verification

  • High-definition image resolution and color balance calibration.
  • Optical light transmission lux output measurement.
  • Suction rate & air/water flow dynamics evaluation.
  • Electrical continuity & video signal stability test across pins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Colonoscope Repair

Essential information on turnarounds, leak testing, OEM specifications, and warranty coverage.

What is the typical repair time for a colonoscope?

Minor servicing (angulation adjustments, sealing) is completed within 24–48 hours. Major rebuilds, such as full insertion tube replacements or bending section overhauls, typically take 3–5 business days. Free loaner scopes are dispatched express nationwide to eliminate procedure cancellations.

How can we identify a leak or fluid invasion in a colonoscope?

Perform a dry-pressure leak test prior to chemical reprocessing. Look for a drop in pressure on the gauge or continuous air bubbles when submerged. If you suspect a leak, do not reprocess the scope; package it immediately and contact us for urgent decontamination and repair.

What colonoscope brands and models do you service?

We service all major flexible colonoscope brands, specializing in Olympus EVIS EXERA II and III (180/190 series, e.g., CF-HQ190L, PCF-H190DL, CF-H190L, PCF-HQ190L) as well as primary platforms from Pentax Medical and Fujifilm.

Are colonoscope bending rubber and bending section repairs done to OEM specs?

Yes. We use medical-grade, biocompatible bending rubbers and high-tensile stainless steel wire mesh to rebuild the bending section. All repairs are calibrated to match original OEM angulation range and deflection force.

Do you offer a warranty on colonoscope repairs?

Yes, all colonoscope repairs include our comprehensive warranty: 90 days for standard repairs and up to 8 months for major overhauls (such as insertion tube replacements or CCD sensors), covering both replaced components and technician craftsmanship.
