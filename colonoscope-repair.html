Colonoscope Repair

Expert repair and rebuild of colonoscopes to OEM standards for optimal performance.

Comprehensive Colonoscope Servicing & Rebuilds

Colonoscopes undergo significant mechanical stress and chemical exposure during daily clinical procedures and reprocessing cycles. At Endoscope Services, we provide high-grade flexible endoscope repairs, servicing, and comprehensive rebuilds to restore your equipment to precise manufacturer specifications. Operating from our workshop in Noble Park, Victoria, our technicians utilize advanced calibration tools and certified testing methods to ensure the highest standards of safety, optical clarity, and electrical isolation.

Common Repairs

  • Insertion Tube Replacement: Fixes kinks, bites, and deep surface scratches using OEM-grade medical materials with RIT (Responsive Insertion Technology) optimization to ensure patient comfort.
  • Bending Section Re-build: Complete replacement of bending section mesh, standard alignment of angulation guide wires, and precise tension calibration to restore standard 4-way control.
  • Biopsy & Suction Channel Replacement: Installation of chemical-resistant, high-durability internal channels to resolve recurring leaks, surface blockages, and fluid bypass issues.
  • Image Sensor (CCD) Repair: Restoration of high-definition video feeds, color balance calibration, and electronic termination stability to eliminate video interference or signal loss.
  • Air/Water Channel Clearing: Flushing and clearing internal blockages, nozzle maintenance, and light guide connector seal restoration to ensure clear field-of-view during procedures.

Specialized Diagnostics & Calibration

  • Pressure & Leak Testing: Exhaustive dry and wet pressure diagnostics to locate micro-fissures and prevent fluid invasion.
  • Light Guide Fiber Bundle Repair: Re-aligning or replacing optical light guide fibers to restore proper distal illumination and eliminate dark spots.
  • Distal End Polishing: Precision buffing and sealing of the distal cap and objective lens to restore optical purity.
  • Video Connector Re-termination: Repairing electrical contacts, internal circuitry, and sealing blocks inside the scope connector.
  • 40-Point Safety Inspection: Every device undergoes a meticulous 40-point verification checklist covering mechanical, optical, and electrical properties before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does colonoscope repair take?

Our standard turnaround time is 3 to 5 business days. We also offer an emergency overnight repair option for urgent cases, subject to parts availability and workshop capacity.

Do you service Olympus, Pentax, and Fujifilm colonoscopes?

Yes, we service all major brands including Olympus (e.g., EVIS EXERA III CF-HQ190L, CF-H190), Pentax, and Fujifilm models to manufacturer-equivalent standards.

What warranty do you provide on colonoscope repairs?

We offer a comprehensive 90-day warranty on all parts and labor associated with the repair, along with our 7-day satisfaction guarantee to ensure total clinical confidence.

Do you offer loaner endoscopes?

Yes, we provide free loaner endoscopes during the repair period to ensure your medical facility maintains its scheduling and operations without clinical downtime.
