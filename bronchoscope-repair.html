Bronchoscope Repair

Specialized services for bronchoscopes used in respiratory diagnostics.

Delicate Bronchoscope Care & Repair Solutions

Bronchoscopes are thin, highly flexible instruments used in critical respiratory diagnostics and interventional pulmonology. Because of their small diameter, they are extremely delicate and susceptible to structural damage and fluid invasion. At Endoscope Services, our specialized workshop in Noble Park, Victoria, provides high-precision repairs for bronchoscopes. Our expert technicians handle these sensitive instruments with maximum care, restoring fine mechanical components and optical systems to original factory standards.

Expert Solutions

  • Angulation & Wire Tuning: Restoring precise 2-way or 4-way angulation control by replacing guidance wires and re-tensioning pulley assemblies.
  • Insertion Tube Rebuilding: Replacing kinked or dented insertion tubes with OEM-equivalent medical-grade materials to ensure smooth entry.
  • Suction & Biopsy Channel Repairs: Repairing or replacing internal channels to resolve suction degradation and internal leakage.
  • Objective Lens Restoration: Polishing micro-scratches from the distal objective lens and re-sealing it to prevent internal fogging.
  • Fiber Optic Bundle Re-alignment: Repairing or replacing light guide fibers to deliver bright, high-intensity illumination.

Our Precision Diagnostic Process

  • Wet Leakage Assessment: Submersion leak testing under continuous pressure to identify seal damage before internal electronics corrode.
  • Angulation Calibration: Adjusting control knobs and cables to match exact manufacturer specifications for steering.
  • Optics Inspection: Reviewing image fibers under high magnification to check for broken pixels or optical misalignment.
  • Disinfection & Safety Test: Thorough cleaning, checking electrical conductivity, and verifying structural integrity before return.
  • Expedited Pulmonology Rebuilds: Prioritized scheduling to return critical pulmonary diagnostic tools to active service quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can bronchoscopes be repaired?

We prioritize bronchoscopes due to the critical nature of respiratory procedures. Minor repairs are usually completed within 2 to 4 business days.

Do you provide loaner bronchoscopes?

Yes, we offer complimentary loaner bronchoscopes during the repair period to minimize any clinical downtime for your pulmonary department.

Can you repair fluid damage or internal corrosion in bronchoscopes?

Yes, our specialized technicians can perform complete camera and light-guide reconstructions, replacing corroded channels and rebuilding sealed connectors.

Which brands of bronchoscopes do you repair?

We service all major diagnostic and therapeutic bronchoscopes, including Olympus, Pentax, and Fujifilm models, aligning with original manufacturer specs.
