Sell Your Equipment

Information on selling and trading medical equipment.

Unlock Value from Retired Equipment

Upgrade your inventory or clear out unused storage by selling your retired or broken flexible endoscopes and surgical power tools to us. We offer competitive market rates based on current demand for refurbished equipment in Australia. Unlike selling overseas or through general auction platforms, you deal directly with an end-buyer who will refurbish and reuse your equipment domestically — meaning faster transactions and no export compliance concerns.

Trade-in Options Available

Looking to upgrade? We offer credit towards repairs or refurbished equipment when you trade in your old scopes. This is particularly useful for facilities upgrading from older Olympus 180-series scopes to current 190-series technology — trade-in credit can significantly offset the purchase price of a certified refurbished replacement.

What We Buy

  • Olympus, Pentax, and Fujinon/Fujifilm flexible endoscopes
  • Broken or non-functional equipment
  • Surplus operational equipment
  • Surgical power tools and consoles
  • Video processors and light sources
  • Irrigation pumps and ancillary equipment

How It Works

  1. Submit a list: Send us the model numbers, serial numbers, and a brief condition description for each item.
  2. Receive a preliminary quote: We review your list and provide a written offer, typically within 2 business days.
  3. Ship to our workshop: If you accept, we provide shipping guidance. Equipment should be safely packaged in its original case if available.
  4. Assessment and confirmation: Upon receipt, we inspect the equipment and confirm the final offer. For items in better or worse condition than described, we will communicate any adjustments before finalising.
  5. Payment: Prompt electronic payment is made once the final offer is accepted. We do not hold payment pending resale.

What Affects the Valuation?

Several factors influence the price we can offer for used endoscopes and medical equipment:

  • Model generation: Current-generation Olympus EVIS EXERA III (190-series) scopes command higher prices than older 160/180-series models.
  • Physical condition: Scopes with intact bending rubber, clean channels, and no corrosion attract higher offers.
  • Functional status: Working scopes are valued higher than non-functional ones, though we buy both.
  • Accessories included: Cases, water bottles, and compatible accessories add value.
  • Repair history: A known service history or recent repairs can support a higher valuation.
